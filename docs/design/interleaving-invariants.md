# Interleaving Invariants

The series harness draws one edit at a time, and the series model has stayed clean through every review because of it. Google recovery, undo on Google calendars and the sync's treatment of rows with local-only data have had hand-written tests only, and every review and sweep since 1.5.4 found a neighbour of the last fix there. So the harness gets an interleaving mode (`run.sh --interleave`): a Google-backed series, then 3 to 8 random steps mixing writes here, changes made at Google by someone else, polls, failures injected into a write, Finish, Put back, undo and redo.

This file is that mode's oracle, written before its generator. Each invariant is a statement of the form "after step X, Y holds", derived from a sentence in the docs and cited, with the steps it applies to and what "equal" means for it. As everywhere in the harness, the checks share nothing with the product: what each side holds is read from the rows and from the stand-in, what a step changed is the harness's own record of the request it sent, and an expectation comes from these statements, never from the product's answer. Seven readings the docs left open were settled by the owner on 2026-11-08 (Settled Readings, below, and decisions.md); the oracle treats them as rules.

## Steps

Each step has a code the invariants and the findings use.

- **Writes here** (through `Events`, the plan API and Undo, as the client sends them): `w-all` a whole-series edit; `w-follow` this and following; `w-delfollow` delete following; `w-answer` an answer (Planned or Maybe) from a day on; `w-this-g` a "this event" edit of fields Google holds (title, place, notes, link, status, date, time, length); `w-this-l` a "this event" edit of local-only fields (tags, people, reminders, attendance, icon, coordinates); `w-this-m` both at once; `w-del1` one occurrence deleted; `w-create` a new event or series.
- **Changes at Google** (`r-*`, made through the stand-in's own API as another client, no `If-Match`, so Google's behaviour from the probe applies): `r-rename`, `r-time`, `r-zone`, `r-length`, `r-rule`, `r-bound` (COUNT or UNTIL), `r-skip+` and `r-skip-` (a day cancelled or brought back), `r-exc+` and `r-exc-` (an exception made, or put back to the series), `r-gonly` (a field only Google holds: attendees, colorId, transparency, visibility, Google's own reminders), `r-delete` (the series deleted).
- **Polls**: `p-delta` (the sync token), `p-whole` (a full list), `p-410` (the token refused, then the full list).
- **Failures**: `f-lost` (the stand-in takes write k, its answer is lost), `f-before` (Google refuses write k before taking it: a permission 403, a 4xx the app does not retry and that tells it nothing was written (a rate-limit 403 or a 429 is "Google is busy" and retried, rule 8); a transport error before sending would be a lost answer to the app, which can't tell the two apart), `f-fatal` (the stand-in takes write k, then an `Error` unwinds the process's transaction, as the recovery tests' `fatalAt` does). Each is a write step with its failure point drawn.
- **Recovery**: `finish`, `putback` (Review's two actions on the `google_half` item).
- **Undo**: `u-entry` (`POST /activity/:id/undo` of a chosen entry), `u-bare` (`POST /undo`), `redo` (an undo's own entry undone by its id).

"A write" below means any `w-*` or `f-*` step, an undo, a redo, Finish or Put back. "Any step" includes the `r-*` steps and polls.

## What Equal Means

- **A day** is a series' identity (its uid, or for the part a split made, the new uid) with the date its occurrence shows on in the series' zone. A day keeps its identity when a change moves its key within that date (a new clock or zone: "a key the series' clock or zone moved goes to the one occurrence on its day", decisions.md, "A local-only 'this event' edit of an ordinary day sends Google nothing"), and when a drag moves it to its image under the oracle (Expect). A day after a split's cut belongs to the new series.
- **Held here alone** (`alone(row)`): what Google never holds, read from the rows and the tables that hang off them: tags (by name, as a set), people (by id, as a set), reminders (the normalized list, null and `[]` distinct), attendance, icon, coordinates and their provenance (`location_lat`, `location_lng`, `location_source`, `location_provider`, `location_placed_at`), trip links (`event_links`, each as its role and the other end's day), the trip flag, the floating flag, duplicate decisions and plugin data. Sources: google-calendar.md, What Writes Send ("Not sent: attendees, reminders and color. Tags, people, reminders and the trip flag stay local and survive polls"), the floating paragraph under it ("keeps the setting here"), series-model.md, Own Fields (`Series\OwnFields::REFERENCES`: trip links, duplicate decisions, plugin data). Coordinates are held here alone, kept while Google's place text is unchanged and dropped when the text changes (A1).
- **The Google digest** of a series (`gdigest`), from the comparison the recovery rule defines (decisions.md, "'Nothing changed since' includes the series' zone": "start and end, the all-day flag, the rule, the content, and the zone of a timed event as the string Google holds"; series-model.md, Google as a Contract, Recovery: "compared field by field with the etag aside"): the master's start and end instants, all-day flag, zone string when timed, rule as parts (list values as sets, `INTERVAL=1` dropped, EXDATE lines as a set of instants), title, description, location text, link (`source.url`) and status; and each instance record by its `originalStartTime`, with the same fields, a cancelled record as a skip. A record whose fields are the series' own values at its occurrence is left out ("an ordinary occurrence here, no edited day", series-model.md, Google as a Contract). Etag, sequence, `updated` and the fields only Google holds are not in it.
- **The full Google state** (`gfull`): the digest plus every field Google holds that the app never sends (attendees, colorId, transparency, visibility, reminders, guest permissions), by series and by record.
- **The digest here** (`ldigest`): the same fields read from the rows of each live series (the master, and each edited row by its key), in the same shape, so `gdigest = ldigest` is a comparison of like with like. It extends `GoogleStand::held` and `stored` with the zone and the rows' zones; both stay the harness's own.
- **The state here** (`lstate`): `ldigest` plus `alone(row)` and `override_fields` (as a set) for every row, by day. Row ids are left out except where an invariant says an id is kept. `updated_at`, `google_etag` and the bookkeeping tables c-untouched names are left out.
- **Named**: a value removed by a step is named when an Activity entry the step wrote (its `summary` or `details`) carries the removed row's id and its shown date in its `details`, with words in its `summary`, or, for Finish and Put back, when an item of their answer's `differences` names it (A5).
- **What a step changed** (`asked(step)`): the harness's record of its own request: the fields it sent with their values, on which rows (a "this event" edit: the day; a whole-series edit: the series and every row not owning the field, with the choices committed; a placement change: the oracle's expectation for it, Expect), and for an undo the fields its entry's step asked. It is never read from the product's snapshots.

## The Invariants

### x-alone: nothing held here alone goes without a word

After any step, every value in `alone(row)` that a day had before the step is still on a row standing for that day, or the step removed it on purpose (it is in `asked(step)`, or the step deleted that day or its series and recorded the removal in its own undoable entry), or the step named it.

Applies after: every step. It is the mode's main check, and the one every sweep shape so far has failed.

From: google-calendar.md, What Writes Send ("Tags, people, reminders and the trip flag stay local and survive polls"; "either way every day keeps what only Better-Cal holds for it: its tags, people, reminders, answer, icon and trip links, and the coordinates picked for the place it shows", of Finish and Put back); How Syncing Works ("if the series no longer has the day ... the day and its tags go, named in the poll's Activity entry"); decisions.md, "Recovery removes only what it made" ("a day with no row left for it is put back as it was and named, never dropped silently"); "A local-only 'this event' edit of an ordinary day sends Google nothing" ("A day the series no longer has is removed with its tags, people and reminders, named in the poll's Activity entry").

Finish and Put back are judged against the rows before the change they settle, so what a store of the half-done change took from a row is a loss like any other: the store's effect is the change's own, never something that happened at Google (decisions.md, "What a store of a half-done change takes from a row is given back by recovery"). A1's exemption still reads the place the day shows after the recovery: a day that shows its own place text again must hold the coordinates picked for it. Fixed cases (seeds 61349 case 272 and 60611 case 153): `a-day-tagged-then-given-coordinates-a-place-for-all-then-a-flip-stopped-after-its-master-write-then-finished`, the same `-then-put-back`, and `a-day-with-reminders-then-its-own-place-with-coordinates-a-time-change-whose-second-answer-was-lost-then-finished`. What the check reads of a row is tags, people, reminders, an answer, an icon, coordinates (not where they came from), the trip and floating flags and trip links by role; duplicate decisions and plugin data are in the statement above and not yet in the reader, and the generator gives a day no trip link, duplicate decision or plugin data, and an answer only through a split, so those cells are the database suite's (google-series-recovery-held).

### x-sync-silent: a poll sends Google nothing

After `p-delta`, `p-whole` or `p-410`, the stand-in took no POST, PATCH or DELETE during the step, and `gfull` is as it was before it.

From: google-calendar.md, intro ("write-through, not two-way sync: each change goes to Google first"); How Syncing Works (a poll lists and stores); decisions.md, "Outside connections do only what changes".

### x-local-silent: a change here alone sends Google nothing

After `w-this-l`, after a change for all events of only fields held here alone, and after an undo or redo of an entry that was such a change, the stand-in took no write and `gfull` is unchanged; the row a `w-this-l` makes for an ordinary day is under its instance key and survives the next poll while the series has the day. A change is local only when it leaves every Google-held field of every series as it is: "this and following" and an answer from a later day are splits at Google whatever fields they carry ("'this and following' ends the old series at Google and inserts the new one", google-calendar.md, What Writes Send; an answer from a day on is among the changes to a whole repeating series that undo by a second write to Google, google-calendar.md, Activity), so x-local-silent doesn't apply to them; from the series' first day they are the whole series' change and it does.

From: decisions.md, "A local-only 'this event' edit of an ordinary day sends Google nothing"; "A change made here alone to a Google event undoes like any local change" ("keep their snapshots and undo here, sending Google nothing"); api-contract.md, Activity ("A change made here alone to an event on a Google calendar ... undoes here as on a local calendar, sending Google nothing").

### x-write-asked: a write changes at Google only what was asked

After a write that succeeds, for every field of `gdigest` that `asked(step)` doesn't reach, Google's value is the one it had before the step (an exception Google dropped on a master change and the app sent again counts as unchanged when it ends equal). Every field of `gfull` outside `gdigest` is unchanged on every series and record, whatever the step, and no request body the stand-in took carries `attendees`, `reminders` or `colorId`.

Applies after: `w-*` but `w-this-l`, `u-entry`, `u-bare`, `redo`, `finish`, `putback`.

From: google-calendar.md, What Writes Send (the list of what is sent; "Not sent: attendees (they would mail people), reminders (per-user at Google) and color"); decisions.md, "The undo of a Google edit sends back only the fields that edit changed"; series-model.md, Google as a Contract, What the app sends ("the fields that changed"). Put back's case is x-putback's.

### x-agree: the rows are what Google holds

After any write that succeeds and after any poll, `ldigest = gdigest` for every live series of the case, and a poll right after (`p-delta`, then `p-whole`) changes no row. After an `r-*` step and before a poll the two may differ, and nothing is asserted.

From: google-calendar.md, intro ("Google's answer is saved here the same way a poll would save it, so there's never a local-only version of a Google event"); series-model.md, Google as a Contract ("Acceptance is the read-back and the sync after it ... a second sync must change nothing"; "Local rows are written from what Google returned, never from the plan"); decisions.md, the local-only rule ("under a whole read and a delta read alike"). A write that Google kept differently is still equal here (the rows are Google's), and x-honest checks that it was named.

### x-follow: a row of local things follows its series

After a poll, a row whose own fields are all held here alone exists exactly when the series, as stored after the poll, has its key (or, for a key the series' clock or zone moved, the one occurrence on its date), and each field it doesn't own (title, place, notes, link, status, the time from its key, all-day, zone) equals the series' value at that occurrence. A row the series no longer has is gone and named (x-alone).

Applies after: `p-*`, chiefly after `r-time`, `r-zone`, `r-length`, `r-rule`, `r-bound`, `r-skip+`, `r-rename`, `r-delete`.

From: decisions.md, "A local-only 'this event' edit of an ordinary day sends Google nothing" ("after the poll it exists only while the series, as stored then, has its key ... and every field it doesn't own takes the series' value"); google-calendar.md, How Syncing Works ("its title, place, notes and time reach that day on the next poll, the day moves with the series' new clock time").

### x-dropped: a dropped record keeps what is held here

After a poll that reads a record of Google's as gone or as plain (dropped by `r-time`, `r-zone` or `r-rule`, overwritten by `r-rename`, put back by `r-exc-`), a day that had a row with values held here alone keeps exactly those values on a row for that day, its Google-held fields now the series' (owned no longer), while the series has the day; else x-follow's removal applies.

From: decisions.md, the local-only rule ("the same rule keeps an edited day whose own fields are all held here ... when a feed, Google or ICS, drops its record of that day"); series-model.md, Rows from Elsewhere ("Changed at the other end: ... inherited when it equals it"; "A person's local changes to such a row ... are its own and kept across polls"); Google as a Contract ("unless that row owns something Google doesn't hold").

### x-structure: one row per day, on the series' days

After any step, every live series of the case has at most one live row per key; every edited row's key and every skip is an occurrence of its series as stored (the harness's evaluator, `Rfc`); no live row has a master that is gone; an undo or redo makes no row for a day its series doesn't have.

From: series-model.md, Own Fields; the harness's invariant h (README); api-contract.md, Activity ("Something the change belonged to deleted since makes it `not_undoable`"); series-model.md, Google as a Contract ("no edited day outlives its series or comes back by a poll").

### x-identity: a day row's stored Google id is its identity

After any step, while no change is half done, every live day row with a Google id whose series has one stores the id of its key under its series' form as stored: `<series id>_<YYYYMMDD>` of the key when the series is all-day (the key is that date at 00:00:00 UTC), `<series id>_<UTC basic time>Z` when it is timed. The harness works the id out with its own two-line function (`Interleave::googleIdOf`), never with `GoogleWriter::instanceId`. It is not skipped for a series changed at Google and not yet polled: the rows here agree among themselves whatever Google holds, and the poll that changes a series' form must give each of its days the new form's id. The database suite and the series mode check the same rule over all data by raw SQL (`identityViolations`), with its zone companions.

From: audit-155-identity, the restated rule ("a day row's stored Google id always equals the identity of its key under its master's form") and invariant I1; series-model.md, Google as a Contract.

### x-own: an edited day follows the series in what it doesn't own

After any write that succeeds and after any poll, every field of an edited row outside its `override_fields` (the date, time, length and all-day placement included, by `OwnFields::placed`'s rule as the harness reads it) equals the series' value at its occurrence. Tags and people are compared as sets: a day that doesn't own them holds exactly its series' set as it stands in the same snapshot (the link rule; the database suite and the series mode check the same rule over all data by raw SQL). After a poll, ownership follows Rows from Elsewhere: a field unchanged at Google keeps its ownership, one changed to the master's value becomes inherited, one changed to another value its own.

From: series-model.md, Own Fields ("A series-wide change reaches every field an edited day doesn't own; its own fields stay"); Rows from Elsewhere (the three rules); decisions.md, "A day kept by an undo shows the series' tags and people it does not own" ("a day's tags and people are its own only when its own list says so; otherwise its links are the series' copy").

### x-conflict: a stale etag is refused whole

After a write of fields Google holds, or an undo of one, sent while Google changed that series since the last poll or write here (an `r-*` step not yet polled), the answer is 409 `google_conflict`, the stand-in took no write that landed, and `lstate` is as before the step.

From: api-contract.md, Google Calendar ("every edit and delete is sent with the event's stored Google etag as `If-Match`, Google answered that the event changed since, and nothing was changed here or there"); Activity ("409 `google_conflict` when the event or series changed at Google since, with nothing written"); series-model.md, Google as a Contract ("the first write is conditional on the stored etag").

A request that changes nothing sends nothing and is no conflict: a step that went through asking Google nothing (no read either: a write that changes something reads the series first) and changing nothing here is not expected refused, whatever field its request names, and is tallied as "nothing to change" (decisions.md, "A request that changes nothing sends nothing and is no conflict", settled 2026-11-09). Before, the check read the request's field label and demanded a refusal of a time change for all events asking the clock the series already had (seed 857 case 249). The x-conflict probe that answers a refused write ok is still caught, since that write's read reached Google. Fixed case: `a-whole-series-clock-the-day-already-owns-changes-nothing-and-is-no-conflict`.

### x-pending: a failure leaves the change listed, or nothing at all

After `f-before` at the first write: an error, the stand-in unchanged, `lstate` unchanged, no `google_pending` row and no `google_half` item. After any write to a series while a change of it is half done (from an earlier step): refused with the pending reason, pointing to Review, nothing written here or at Google (A6). After any other failure (`f-lost`, `f-fatal`, or `f-before` after an earlier write landed): the answer fails with `pending`; Review lists one `google_half` item for it whose `stepsDone` covers every write the stand-in took, the one whose answer was lost included; x-alone holds.

From: series-model.md, Google as a Contract, Recovery ("The pending row is written on a connection of its own, so it is committed before the first request whatever the account's transaction does after"; "A transaction rolled back with nothing sent takes it too"; "The request that stopped fails with the error and `pending`"); api-contract.md, Review (`google_half`, `stepsDone`: "a step whose answer never came included").

### x-finish: Finish completes the change over what stands now

After `finish` that answers ok: for every field the pending change asked (`asked` of the failed step, the local part included: tags, people, reminders and flags, review R3), the rows and Google hold the change's value; for every other field, they hold the value from just before Finish; every place where either side departs from that is named in `differences`. A day changed at Google since that the change did not touch is kept, or, where the change's own write to the series had been taken (so what that write did to the days can't be told from a change made there since), it is the change's again and `differences` says what Finish replaced there, with what Google held, under its lead sentence. A row made here since the failure keeps its id and what it holds, with the plan applied over it; a row the change's own stores made is gone, or, holding something held here alone since, has handed it to the row left for its day. The pending row and the Review item are gone. After `finish` that answers 409 `google_finish_local`: nothing held here alone is lost and the item stays listed.

"The change's value" for a field change is the value sent; for a placement change (time, zone, rule, bound, a split) it is Expect's expectation for the edit, read from the series as it was when the change was planned, and is asserted only when nothing at Google changed since; with a change at Google since, the digest comparison is equal-or-named.

What Finish keeps of changes made at Google since (A4) is Google's state when Finish runs, not the first remote step's value: each day a remote step since the failure touched in a field the change did not ask holds at Google after Finish what it held just before Finish, its record and whether it is skipped. So a later remote step on the same day supersedes an earlier one: a day given an exception and then cancelled at Google is skipped after Finish, and one cancelled and then edited holds what Google holds. Before, A4 expected the remote title on a live record for every remote title or notes touch and never asked whether a later remote step cancelled that day (seed 60611 case 143). Fixed cases: `a-split-stopped-then-a-day-given-an-exception-and-cancelled-at-google-then-finished-is-put-back-and-named`, and `a-split-stopped-then-a-day-given-an-exception-at-google-then-finished-is-replaced-and-named`, whose probe drops the day's record after Finish.

From: google-calendar.md, What Writes Send ("Finish (the rest of it, from what Google has now, then its part here: tags, people, reminders and flags)"); series-model.md, Recovery ("Finish runs the same targets from Google's state, then writes the change's part here ... a row made here since ... stays with what it has, and the plan is applied over it"); decisions.md, "Put back is all or nothing" ("Finish writes the local part of a mixed change from the plan kept with the pending row"); "Recovery removes only what it made".

The hidden-days rule (decisions.md, "A switch between timed and all-day that Google would hide days with is refused"): a Finish of a switch between timed and all-day that Google would hide a day with, by the harness's own reading of the stand-in (`GoogleStand::hidesOnSwitch`), is refused with `google_hidden_days` before anything is sent; that is an accepted outcome, with nothing sent and the change left in Review. A Finish refused so where the harness's reading finds no day hidden fails x-finish. That Put back may then be refused too, as changed at Google since, is a known limit, not judged here.

### x-putback: Put back is all or nothing, and the harness knows which

The harness decides on its own whether a target series changed at Google since the failure: for a series whose last step was answered, any `r-*` step on it since (Google changes the etag on every write, probe 5.1); for one whose last step's answer was lost, an `r-*` step since that changed its `gdigest`. "Since" counts from the half-done change's last step: a change made at Google before the failing change, polled or not, is part of what the change started from (it read the series before its first write), and Put back may run while Google holds what the change started from or what its steps left. Before, the harness seeded the record at the failure with any unpolled change made at Google before it, so a guest added there refused Put back in its expectation (seed 60611 case 271, seed 7717 case 108). Fixed cases: `a-guest-added-at-google-then-notes-for-all-stopped-after-their-write-then-put-back-goes-through`, and `a-guest-added-at-google-then-notes-for-all-stopped-then-a-colour-at-google-then-put-back-is-refused`, whose probe answers that Put back ok.

After `putback` with a series changed since: 409 `google_changed_since`, `left` names exactly the series changed since, the stand-in took no write, `lstate` is unchanged and the item stays listed. With none changed: ok with `left: []`; every target series' `gfull` at Google is what it was before the failed change (an inserted series cancelled); the rows here are as they were before the change, but for what was made here since, which stays (a row a store made that was given something held here alone hands it to the day's row); the pending row and the item are gone.

From: google-calendar.md, What Writes Send ("Put back (every step sent, reversed, a step whose answer never came included, all or nothing: only while nothing changed at Google since; if anything did, even in a series this change inserted, nothing is put back"); api-contract.md, Review (`put_back`, `left`); decisions.md, "Put back is all or nothing"; "'Nothing changed since' includes the series' zone". A change at Google to a field only Google holds is A2.

### x-undo-fields: an undo takes back only what its entry changed

After `u-entry`, `u-bare` or `redo` that answers ok, every field in `asked` of the entry's step is back at its value from before that step (for redo, from after it), and every other field, here and at Google, is as it stood before the undo (a change made since in another field, a remote one read by a poll included, stays). When a later entry not undone asked the same field on the same row (or, for a repeating event, changed its times where this entry deleted, edited or split off a date, or the other way round), the answer is `stale_undo` and nothing is written anywhere. When the trip rules refuse what it puts back, `undo_conflict` and nothing is written.

Since the 2026-11-09 review the check is exact, against the rows as they were before the edit: an undo of a field edit puts each field the edit set back at the value it had then (on a day the edit made its own, the series' value as it stands now when the day didn't own the field before), a redo puts the edit's value back, and every other field of every row, and every row but the edit's own day, is as it was before the undo or redo. Before, the check asked only that the field differ from the value the edit set, and never looked at a redo's content (review of 56e62890 to 9de4e3dc). "The same field on the same row" is the day's own field: a field a day's row shows from its series, owned neither as the entry left it nor now, is the series', and a later change of the series' value does not make the day's entry stale, whether or not the row is still there for something else it holds (a day kept for its reminders, its rename undone, the series renamed, the rename redone: the redo goes through; seed 54249 case 109, 2026-11-10, where the check compared the title the row showed). From: decisions.md, "The undo of a Google edit sends back only the fields that edit changed" ("a field a later entry changed again is stale as on a local calendar; redo carries the same fields"); api-contract.md, Activity (`stale_undo`, `undo_conflict`; a `series` entry "each series as putting the entry's snapshots back here leaves it, so what changed since in other fields stays"); series-model.md, Undo for Google series.

Since the review of 9de4e3dc to 8e568077 the check is judged against states recorded when the entry was made, never inferred from the chain of entries. Each Activity entry's rows are recorded by identity (a series by its Google id, a day by its series and key, a single event by its Google id; row ids are ignored, since a recreated row takes a new one) before and after the step that made it. An undo of that entry is expected to leave what the entry changed as it was before (rows it made gone, rows it removed back with their values then, each field it changed at its value then, the own lists as the flipped entry implies) and a redo as it was after; every other row's own fields and own list, and every row the entry never touched, are as they stood before the undo or redo. Staleness is one ground model, as the decisions state it (decisions.md, "Staleness has one definition: grounds are fields" and "An undo of a Google series change is planned as a forward change is", settled 2026-11-09; api-contract.md, `stale_undo`): an entry is stale when its ground and a later change's meet. An entry's ground is the fields it changed: a column of a row; a day's own field, the day counted by its series and occurrence and followed through a later times or zone change that carried it (the harness reads every judgement of an entry in the frame of the rows before it, each day of a series whose clock or zone moved renamed to its occurrence there, its skips with it), so a series times change never stales an entry about a day, whose undo carries the day to its occurrence now; where it made or removed a day's row whole, the fields that row owns, its tags and people among them, never a value the row only shows from its series (a title, link or place a series change or a poll wrote); a skip per date, never the skips list whole; and the own list is never a ground itself, its contents are. The days a change of a series' rule, bound or start added are its ground too, so a row made or changed at an added date since stales its undo (decisions.md, "An undo of a Google series change is planned as a forward change is"; fixed case `a-count-raise-then-an-added-day-edited-then-its-undo-is-stale`). A date skipped again over a row made there since removes that row whole, so it stands on the fields that row owns. An undo of an entry nothing has changed since goes through: the only refusals the oracle accepts then are `google_pending` while a change is half done, `not_undoable` from the release gate, and Google's refusal of a series changed there and not yet read (x-conflict's); any other refusal fails with its code in the signature (decisions.md, "An undo is stale when a poll has since brought a remote change of the same field"; a refusal saying the event was no longer there hid a defect until this rule). From: case 2 ("Your change goes through; Google's value for the other field stays, here and there"), case 4, case 6, (d).
- The request budget: one read of the event, and one conditional write when something is left to send, none after a question, at most one when nothing is left. From: case 1 ("one read, one conditional write"), case 3 ("Nothing is sent"), case 7 ("no question, nothing left to send"). x-conditional still asks that write carry `If-Match`.
- Overwrite, sent right after the question as (c) says, with nothing changed at Google since, goes through, and the general expectation then holds the person's values at both sides. From: case 3 ("Overwrite sends your change again and it goes through"), (c) ("if Google still holds that value, the change goes through").
- No other row here and nothing else at Google changes.

A question or a change that goes through stores what Google holds for that event, so the harness counts the event read, as a poll would, and x-agree then compares it with the stand-in (the page's step: "the read stored through `Feeds::sync` ... as a poll stores it").

**x-undo-fields, after a change over a stored read.** The read is stored "as a poll does, with its own Activity entry" (api-contract.md, Google Calendar), so the change's own entry starts from the rows with the read stored, not from the rows before the step: the harness records that state as the entry's before (the store's own entry, a source of Google's, keeps the rows before the step, and no step here undoes it). Its undo then puts back what the change itself changed and leaves a field Google changed as Google has it, as the undo of any change does over an earlier poll. Before, the first run of seed 7 with the conflict choice on judged the entry from the rows before the read and expected a link (case 119) and a place (case 264) that Google had changed, to the very value the change also set, put back to what was shown before Google's change: a check defect, the harness's model of one state before a step's entries. Fixed case: `an-event-in-no-series-given-its-new-link-at-google-and-here-then-undone-keeps-googles-link`, whose probe puts the old link back after the undo.

**A change the row already holds asks Google nothing.** The step runs "only when the request sends Google something (a non-empty `patchBody`, a delete, an answer)" (the page, (e)), and "A request that changes nothing sends nothing and is no conflict" (decisions.md). So a `w-single` whose every field the row here already holds (a poll stored Google's Maybe, and the person, still shown Planned, clicks Maybe) is expected to go through with no read and no write, and the three-way comparison is not asked of it. Before, x-choice expected one read whenever the request differed from what was shown (seed 94756 case 266): a check defect.

**An event in no series made or removed whole stands on every field.** x-undo-fields' staleness read an event of its own made by `w-create` as a day row, whose ground is the fields it owns, so the undo of an event's making after the event was made Maybe was expected to go through (seed 94756 case 79). An event in no series is no day: its making changed every column of its row, and "a row or series made whole has every field of what it made as its ground" (above, x-undo-fields; decisions.md, "Staleness has one definition: grounds are fields"). Before 1.5.7 nothing wrote a `w-create` event again, so the gap never showed.

**x-agree, extended.** The case's events in no series (`w-create`'s) are in both digests, by the six fields, so after any write that went through and any poll the rows here hold what Google holds for them too, and an event Google holds with no row here fails.

**Probes.** Eleven oracle probes damage what x-choice reads, one per statement (a kept title put back at Google, `kept` left out of the answer, a sent place not written here, a second read, a question answered as done, the read not stored, the request's tag written on a question, a wrong `now`, Overwrite leaving Google's title, Overwrite asked again, a delete's question missing its field), one gives an event in no series another title here than at Google after a change (x-agree), and one puts back after the undo a link Google changed (x-undo-fields). Six fixed cases (`cases/interleave/an-event-in-no-series-*`) hold the page's cases 2, 3 with Overwrite, 5, 6 with Delete and 7, and the undo after a stored read.

**A time set in part is read against what was shown (the review of 2026-11-14, finding 3).** The review reproduced what the harness could not draw: a timed event shown 18:00 to 19:00, made all-day at Google and stored by a poll, then dragged by the stale client (start and end with the clocks, `over` the shown time, no `allDay`, as the app's drag sends it) was asked about, and overwritten, as a timed event from midnight to midnight; and a start sent alone after a poll stored 20:00 to 21:00 borrowed 21:00. Its note: "The harness's remote single-event time change shifts timed endpoints without changing event kind." So the single shape now draws, in the same steps: an all-day event made (one to three days, 30%); Google's time change shifted whole, by its start, by its end, or by its kind (timed to all-day over one to three days, all-day to timed), and moved onto the time the change here sets half the time it would not be by chance; a poll between twice as often when the kind changed, with the request carrying the record shown before it (stale) or the one served after it, recorded in the case's `single` line; and the change here as a drag (start and end, no allDay), the app's resize (the shown start and a new end), an end alone, a start alone, or the editor's all-day switch (start, end and allDay). The forms are the client's own (`web/src/app/actions.js`, `moveEvent` and `resizeEvent`; `web/src/ui/monthmath.js`, which sends an all-day bar's ends as dates; `EditorDrawer.js`, which sends the time row whole), read to learn what the caller sends, never as an expectation.

x-choice now resolves what the change sets (W) from what was shown (B) and the request's parts, never from the row: the kind is the request's `allDay` where sent, else the shown kind; each end sent is read in that kind, an all-day end as its date; each end not sent is the shown one. From: the page's (c), "a request that set only part of the time (a drag sends `start` and `end`) was read against what the person was shown when it was first sent, and sent again unchanged it would be read against what Google holds now (a drag to six o'clock over an event Google made all-day would become all-day)"; (b), "the value in the request's `over` ... else the row's value", with its reason, "a poll can store Google's change while the editor is open"; case 5, "the comparison is with what you were shown, not with what the row holds now"; api-contract.md, Time Zones and All-Day Events, "All-day `start`/`end` on writes ... are dates ... only its date is read". With W read so, the statements above apply unchanged: the question's `yours` is W (now checked on its own, apart from `was` and `now`), a change that goes through holds W here and at Google, and, added, Overwrite lands exactly the `yours` the question showed, here and at Google (the page's (c): "Overwrite sends what the question showed as yours").

Contract undefined: a start sent alone. Neither the page nor api-contract.md (PATCH /events/:id) says whether the end not sent stays where it was shown or keeps the shown length from the new start, so x-choice accepts either reading of what was shown and fails an end from the row. Where Google already holds one of the two readings and not what was shown, the other reading would ask, so nothing is asserted (r-single's "the same value" is never drawn for a start alone). A change of kind with an end left out has no stated reading either; it is not drawn. Three probes: a stale drag's `yours` read against the row, a start alone asked about with an end that is neither reading, and Overwrite landing another time than `yours`; their two fixed cases are answered right by the product with or without the fix the review asked for.

**x-alone, an event in no series whose date moves.** x-alone keys what is held here alone by the day it stands for, which is right for a day of a series (the day is its identity) and wrong for an event of its own, which is its row: before these draws the block never moved such an event off its date, so the gap never showed. The first run of seed 7 with them reported the event's own tag as gone without a word when a poll stored Google's new date or a drag here moved it (cases 64, 194 and 294, and the all-day fixed case's Overwrite): a check defect. An event in no series now keeps an item while its row still holds it, wherever its date went; it goes only when its row no longer holds it, which the rest of x-alone judges as before. From x-alone's own statement: nothing held here alone goes without a word, and nothing went.

**Left undefined, or not reached.** The page answers what a change to one day of a series is ("not in slice 1: 1.5.6 behaviour"), so nothing there is undefined. Not reached by the generator: an answer to an invitation (case 8: the stand-in's events have no attendees the account is a guest of), a single event made repeating at Google, deleted at Google, or changed there between the read and the write (the unlikely list), and the 409's sentence (its words are the database suite's).

## Oracle Probes

A check that passes says something only if it fails on what it claims to catch. Since the review of 9de4e3dc to 8e568077 every check has at least one probe in `tools/edit-harness/OracleProbes.php`: a fixed case that passes, and damage injected after one of its steps into what that step's checks read (the rows, the requests the stand-in took, the answer, the Activity entries, Review), the kind the check's contract names: a renamed or deleted unrelated row, a lost own field, a stale field, a missing `If-Match`, a write carrying a key only Google holds, an advanced sync token, a missing notice, a refused write answered ok, an undo entry left not undone. `run.sh --interleave --oracle-probes` runs each probe's case clean and damaged and passes a probe when its check fails more often on the damaged run. A check with no probe that passes is unvalidated, and every interleaving run's summary lists the checks no probe covers.

## Settled Readings

The first draft listed seven readings the docs left open; the owner settled each on 2026-11-08, and the product-facing ones are in decisions.md under Sync and Storage. The oracle checks them as rules.

- **A1, coordinates.** Coordinates are held here alone: kept while Google's place text is unchanged, dropped when the text changes. A step after which the day shows another place text may take them, whoever changed the text: the step's own request, or Google dropping the day's record so the day shows its series' text again (x-alone and x-dropped exempt them then); a poll that brings the same text back may not. From decisions.md, "A feed's change to a day keeps what is held here": the row keeps "coordinates picked for the place text it shows", and "a new place text clears them". Until 2026-11-09 the checks exempted coordinates only when the step's own request changed the place, which reported a day whose record Google dropped as a loss.
- **A2, what "nothing changed since" covers.** Every field of the Google event resource except Google's own volatile metadata (etag, updated, sequence, htmlLink, iCalUID, kind, creator and organizer stamps), so a change to attendees, colorId, transparency or visibility at Google since a lost answer refuses Put back. The harness counts any change made at Google to a series of the change after the failure as a change since (x-putback), and none made before it.
- **A3, an undo after a poll brought a remote change of the same field.** `stale_undo`, as a later local change would make it (x-undo-fields).
- **A4, Finish over a change made at Google since.** Finish keeps a remote change to any field of the series the change did not touch, and to any day while the change's own write to the series has not been taken; once it has, the days are the change's and Finish says what it replaced (x-finish; decisions.md, "Finish sets the plan's days where it can't tell its own change from another"). What it keeps is Google's state when Finish runs, so a later remote step on the same day supersedes an earlier one.
- **A5, what "named" means.** The row's id and its shown date in the entry's `details`, with words in its `summary`; for Finish and Put back, an item of the answer's `differences`. A removal named by its date alone is reported as such.
- **A6, a write while a change is half done.** A write to a series while a change of it is half done is refused with the pending reason, pointing to Review (x-pending).
- **A7, a process killed outright.** Stays with the database suite's kill test; the harness throws an `Error` after the stand-in took the write, as the recovery tests' `fatalAt` does (`f-fatal`).

## As Built

The generator follows the statements above with these narrowings, each a choice of what to draw rather than a weaker check:

- Placement changes (a whole-series time or length change, a COUNT) are drawn from the series' first day only and checked by the relational invariants (x-alone, x-agree, x-structure, x-own); x-finish asserts the change's values for field changes (title, notes, place, link, tags), where its value is the value sent. Expect's placement oracle is the edit harness's, not repeated here.
- x-agree runs after a write or poll only when no series of the case changed at Google unpolled and no change is half done; the poll it adds (a delta, then a whole read) is the second sync the contract names.
- x-structure, x-own and x-follow skip a series changed at Google since the last poll, which the next poll settles.
- x-alone is deferred on a failure that leaves a change half done (the pending row holds what it took) and judged at Finish or Put back against the rows before the change: Put back may remove nothing, Finish what the change's own request reaches.

## Not Covered

A day past the 730th occurrence of a series with no end (the product leaves it as Google has it and names it; the `--google` mode covers it), invitations organized by someone else (their answer is Google's `responseStatus`, a different path), a series several times a day and a rule the expander can't read (neither exists on a Google calendar, `GoogleStand::unheld`), and moves between calendars (refused on Google).
