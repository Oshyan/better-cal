# The probes still owed, asked of real Google (2026-11-16 and 2026-11-17)

Run with the probe's group 12 (`tools/google-probe`, cases 12.1 to 12.11) on an empty throwaway calendar whose zone is America/Los_Angeles, every write with `sendUpdates=none`, and the app's own requests for a move to Google replayed beside it. Every event was invented ("Probe owed ...", "Probe undo"), dated in 2027 and later, marked, and deleted at the end; the one guest was an invented address at a reserved domain. The write-up says what was seen, in general terms. Each case is listed through the list by the event's iCalUID (`singleEvents=false`, deleted shown) before and after its writes.

## What was seen

- **12.1, a link changed over an edited day (P7).** A weekly series of four with a link, its second day renamed; the master's link changed by a write of the link alone. The renamed day's record holds the new link, and keeps its own title. A link written to a series reaches every kept day, as a set link did in 3.32.
- **12.2, a rule change that leaves a held day out (P8).** A weekly series of ten, its fourth day renamed and its sixth deleted; the rule changed to every other week. Both records are still listed with deleted shown, now cancelled and without `originalStartTime`, and a GET of either id answers 200 cancelled; without deleted shown neither is listed. The rule came back with its parts in Google's own order (`COUNT` before `INTERVAL`).
- **12.3, a single event given a rule by the API (P3).** The PATCH answered 200 with the same id and iCalUID and the rule; the list by that iCalUID holds one item, carrying the rule, and the event's instances are its four weeks. The same as P3 through Google's own page (2026-11-14).
- **12.4, the last remaining day deleted on its own (issue 272).** A weekly series of three; two days deleted one by one, then the third deleted by its own id (204). Google cancelled the event itself: the master reads cancelled and no instance is listed. A PATCH of that day back to confirmed answered 200 and made the day's record confirmed, but the event stayed cancelled. A PATCH of the event to confirmed (with its rule) brought it back with all three days, the two deleted earlier included. Unlike a rule change that leaves only deleted days (3.62), the event can be brought back.
- **12.5, a day's record that names no zone, written with no times (replay round 6).** A daily series in UTC, its start moved a day, so its records' times name no zone. A title written to one record with no times: the answer names the calendar's zone, and so do two later lists. The same after a write of status cancelled. A record not written keeps naming none.
- **12.6, a deleted series confirmed by a write with no rule.** A weekly series of ten with a renamed and a deleted day, the series deleted, then a PATCH of a new title and status confirmed with no rule in the body. Taken: the series is live again with all ten days, the new title written into both records, the deleted day live again too.
- **12.7, the guest list written alone over a day with its own notes.** A weekly series of four with one guest, its second day given its own notes; the guest list written alone to the series, the guest now marked accepted. The day keeps its notes. Its record takes the series' guest list, accepted too. A guest written without an answer came back marked `needsAction`.
- **12.8, a cancel and a delete sent to a record that is no longer a day.** A renamed day left out by a rule change to every other week: a PATCH of status cancelled to its record answered 400, a DELETE 410 "Resource has been deleted". The record is unchanged.
- **12.9, days past the 730th listed occurrence.** A daily series with no end: a write to its 801st and 803rd days by their ids answered 404 each, so no record was made there, and none was listed after a rule change to every other day. A GET of either id answered 200 with the series' own values.
- **12.10, a day deleted by an EXDATE line written to by its id.** A weekly series of ten with an EXDATE line for its third day: a GET of that day's id answered 404, and so did a PATCH to confirmed and a PATCH with its own title and times. Nothing changed; nine instances. Google lists the EXDATE line before the RRULE line.
- **12.11, two events with one identity.** Never seen. An import of an iCalUID a live event holds updates that event in place and answers its id, for a single event and a repeating one, with the same or other content, a new rule included. An insert carrying an iCalUID a live event holds answers 409 "The requested identifier already exists." An import after that event was deleted answers 200 and brings back the same id, confirmed, with the new content. After every step the list by the iCalUID held one item.

## The move to Google with a day of the other form

The app's own requests for moving a calendar of one's own to Google, holding a timed weekly series with one all-day day and an all-day weekly series with one timed day (two imports, each day as one PATCH over the occurrence the import made, the other form's fields null, then a poll), were recorded against the stand-in and replayed on the same calendar: 12 requests, each answered as the stand-in answered.

## What changed because of it

The stand-in (`tools/google-probe/lib/FakeGoogle.php`) answers as above for 12.4, 12.7 and 12.11, and its notes for 12.5 say what was seen ([decisions.md](../../decisions.md), "The stand-in answers a deleted last day, a guest list and a second identity as Google did"). The edge-case table records the last day, two identities and the move as settled ([google-edge-cases.md](../../google-edge-cases.md), rows 7, 43 and 44).

## Limits

One account, one calendar, one day. A day cancelled by a PATCH of status cancelled (not a DELETE) as the last remaining day, and an insert carrying an iCalUID that only a deleted event holds, were not tried.
