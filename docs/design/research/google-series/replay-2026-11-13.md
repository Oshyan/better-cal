## Round 1: the Answer to an Insert

Evidence: `workfolder/google-replay/replay-2026-11-13T093625+0100.json` (every scenario's first divergence, with the request and both answers), checked against the probe of the same morning, `workfolder/google-probe/probe-2026-11-13T091712+0100.json` (3,244 event resources in Google's answers, calendar in America/Los_Angeles).

What the replay reported, after its normalizing: `description` and `recurrence` in 46 scenarios (a timed series or single with a location), `description` alone in 1 (a series with a location and a rule), `description` and `location` in 1 (the split's series, no location), and `description`, `location`, `recurrence` and `reminders` in 1 (an all-day single). The app's insert body sends `description: ""`, `location: ""` when it has none and `recurrence: []` on a single event; the stand-in stored the body and echoed it.

What Google does, as rules:

- **A field sent empty is not in the answer.** `description: ""`, `location: ""` and `recurrence: []` sent on an insert come back absent (all 49 first divergences). Of 3,244 resources in the probe's answers none holds an empty string in summary, description or location, and none holds a null anywhere, though the probe sent `source: null` seven times.
- **Every resource carries `reminders`.** With none sent: `{"useDefault": true}` on a timed event, `{"useDefault": false}` on an all-day one (the replay's all-day single; in the probe, 422 all-day resources, every one false, and 2,652 timed ones true). The replay hid the timed case, since it ignored `{"useDefault": true}`.
- **Every resource carries `eventType: "default"`** (3,244 of 3,244), cancelled ones included. The replay hid it.
- **A `dateTime` is written with the calendar's offset, not the event's zone.** A series in UTC at 18:00Z comes back `2027-05-03T11:00:00-07:00` with `timeZone: "UTC"` kept (the replay's `app-series-deleted-whole`); in the probe, every `start`, `end` and `originalStartTime` `dateTime` carries -08:00 or -07:00 whatever its `timeZone` (UTC 94, America/Denver 42, America/Los_Angeles 2,686). The replay hid it, since it compared instants.
- Also in Google's answer and not compared, by design: `updated` with milliseconds (`.761Z`; the stand-in writes `.000Z`), `created`, `etag`, `htmlLink`, `creator` (the account) and `organizer` (the calendar). These differ between any two runs.

### Errors

From the probes of 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-13: a 400 "Invalid start time." has reason `invalid`; a 404 "Not Found" `notFound`; a 410 "Resource has been deleted" `deleted`; each in `{"error": {"errors": [{"domain": "global", "reason", "message"}], "code", "message"}}`. **A PATCH's 412 is worded differently from a DELETE's:** "Precondition check failed.", reason `failedPrecondition`, with `"status": "FAILED_PRECONDITION"` (four in the 2026-11-13 probe); a DELETE's 412 is "Precondition Failed" (probe of 2026-11-07, 5.5 and 5.7). Not yet seen live, and left as the stand-in had them: the reason of a DELETE's 412, of a 409 on an id taken (the documentation says `duplicate`) and of a 400 for a refused rule.

### Where each difference went

The stand-in should answer as Google does; the replay's normalizing is only for what differs between any two runs (ids, etags, times of writing). So:

- **The stand-in** (`FakeGoogle::shapedAnswer` and `shaped`, applied to every answer on its way out, the store unchanged): a null field, an empty description or location and an empty recurrence are left out; `reminders` is added when none is held, true on a timed event and false on an all-day one; `eventType: "default"` and `kind` always; every `dateTime` in start, end and originalStartTime is written with the calendar's offset at that instant; errors carry the reasons above, and a PATCH's 412 Google's wording.
- **The replay** no longer ignores `reminders {"useDefault": true}`, `eventType "default"` or a `dateTime`'s offset, and compares `kind`. It still ignores `etag`, `updated`, `created`, `sequence`, `htmlLink`, `creator`, `organizer` and the order of an RRULE's parts.

### What the product does with it

The database suite against the reshaped stand-in: 21 assertions fail in four files that all pass before it (`sync-form-recovery`, `google-series-recovery-held`, `google-series-identity-recovery`, `google-series-identity`). Every one is a Put back after a change between timed and all-day stopped after its master write, refused with 409 `google_changed_since` ("changed at Google since"). With the `reminders` shaping alone taken out they pass again; the offsets and the empty fields change nothing in the product's tests. The reading: Put back's "nothing changed since" compares every field of the series as Google gives it, `reminders` among them, against what the stopped step left as the app expects it; on a switch the stand-in's answer now carries another `reminders` (true on the timed form, false on the all-day one), which the app did not send and does not expect, so it reads the series as changed at Google. For a switch from timed to all-day this is what Google is shown to do (no all-day resource in the probe has `useDefault: true`), so on Google, Put back after such a step should be expected to refuse in the same way: a product finding, reported, not adjusted. For a switch from all-day to timed Google keeps `false` (below), so those failures come from the stand-in not yet modelling that, and on Google this direction would not trip on `reminders`.

### Behaviour, not shape: handed back

These are what Google keeps or changes, not how it writes an answer, so they are left to the worker on leftovers and rule changes:

- **`reminders.useDefault` stays false after an event is switched to all-day and back.** In the probe, 170 timed resources carry `{"useDefault": false}`, all in cases where the event or the day was all-day at some point (3.23 to 3.26, 3.29, 3.30, 3.36 to 3.40); none in any other case. The stand-in's shaping gives a timed event true whenever it holds no reminders, so after a switch back it answers true where Google answers false. Expected to show in the next round in a harness scenario that switches back.
- **A day's old all-day id under a timed series answers as a cancelled stub** (probe 3.39, steps 21, 27 and 33, `GET <id>_20270308`): `summary: "CANCELLED"`, `created: "0000-12-31T00:00:00.000Z"`, the calendar as creator, no description, location or private properties. Part of issue 256's leftovers.

### Not checked

- An answer to a PATCH, a GET and a list from the app's own scenarios: every scenario stopped at its first insert. The probe's answers to those requests are the evidence for the shaping above until the next round.
- The top level of a list answer (`summary`, `accessRole`, `defaultReminders`, `updated`): not compared by the replay, not reshaped.
- RRULE part order: the replay still ignores it; the probe saw Google reorder parts, and the order it uses is not yet worked out.
